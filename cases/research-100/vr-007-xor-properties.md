## Analysis workflow
1. Read the official challenge statement and identify its inputs and expected output.
2. Identify the encoding, mathematical operation, or cryptographic primitive involved.
3. Reproduce the minimum transformation required in an authorized challenge environment.
4. Validate the result on the official platform.
5. Record the lesson and defensive implications.

## Defensive takeaway
Use standardized, reviewed cryptographic primitives and maintained libraries. Treat encodings as representations rather than security controls, validate inputs, and keep implementation assumptions explicit.
